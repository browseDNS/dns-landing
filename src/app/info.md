## What is this?
BrowseDNS is a set of DNS servers that enable access to the Internet across different wifi-enabled devices.

By entering one of our DNS addresses in your network settings, you can gain access to the built-in browser on your device.

All modern Internet-capable devices that include a web browser should allow the user to also browse the Internet! Our service helps enable this use-case.
